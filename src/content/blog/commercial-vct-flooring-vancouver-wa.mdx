## 1. Vinyl Composition Tile (VCT) for High-Traffic Commercial Facilities

VCT has remained the gold standard for heavy commercial spaces due to its incredible structural density and low upfront cost:

* **Extreme Wear Tolerance:** VCT tiles consist of natural limestone filler, color pigments, and thermoplastic resins compressed under high pressure. They resist heavy rolling loads, pallet jacks, and high foot traffic.
* **Cost Efficiency:** At a fraction of the per-square-foot cost of other materials, VCT offers unmatched value for large-footprint facilities like elementary schools, supermarkets, and warehouse offices in Vancouver WA.
* **Custom Patterning:** Interlocking grid installation allows custom multi-colored patterns, safety walkways, and corporate accent borders.

## 3. Subfloor Moisture Testing on Concrete Slabs

Many commercial developments along Columbia River waterfront areas in Vancouver feature concrete slab-on-grade construction. Before laying commercial adhesives:

1. **In-Situ Relative Humidity (RH) Probe Testing:** We test slab moisture levels according to ASTM F2170 standards.
2. **Moisture Mitigation:** If RH levels exceed manufacturer limits, we apply two-part epoxy moisture mitigation membranes to prevent adhesive saponification or tile lifting.
