## 3. Sand & Finish vs. Screen & Recoat: Which Do You Need?

Not every floor requires deep sanding down to raw timber:

* **Screen & Recoat (Buff & Coat):** If your floor's wood underneath is healthy and only the top clear coat has light surface scuffs, we lightly abrade the surface and apply a fresh topcoat in just 1 day.
* **Full Sand & Finish:** Required if the floor has deep scratches, water discoloration stains, uneven board wear, or if you want to change the stain color completely.
