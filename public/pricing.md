## Service Categories & Scope

### 1. Solid & Engineered Hardwood Installation
- **Scope:** Full installation of pre-finished or unfinished solid hardwood, engineered timber, moisture barriers, baseboards, and transitional trims.
- **Warranty:** Full installation warranty + manufacturer material warranty.

### 2. Hardwood Sand & Finish Refinishing
- **Scope:** HEPA dust-controlled sanding (captures 98%+ dust), custom color stain matching, low-VOC water-based or oil-modified polyurethane protective topcoats.
- **Buff & Recoat:** Available for minor surface refreshes.

### 3. Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) & Laminate
- **Scope:** 100% waterproof rigid-core LVP/LVT and heavy-commercial laminate floating or glue-down installation with sound-dampening underlayment.

### 4. Professional Carpet Installation
- **Scope:** High-density 8lb padding, power-stretching installation, seam bonding, stairs, plush residential broadloom, and commercial carpet tiles.

### 5. Ceramic & Porcelain Tile
- **Scope:** Floor tiling, backsplashes, custom tile showers, mud pans, waterproofing membranes, and epoxy/cementitious grout.

### 6. Commercial VCT & Sheet Vinyl
- **Scope:** Heavy-traffic vinyl composition tile, heat-welded sheet vinyl, adhesive scraping, and commercial subfloor leveling.
